Test a payment notification signature

What is this?

When someone pays, Polar sends your app an automatic message (a webhook) signed with a secret, so your app can tell it's genuine. Who it's for: developers connecting Polar to an app. When you'd use it: your app keeps rejecting Polar's notifications and you want to know if the secret, timestamp or message body is the problem. The check happens entirely in this page using your browser's built-in cryptography, with no network requests. Even so, prefer a sandbox or test secret.
