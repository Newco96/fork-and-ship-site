Webhook signature tester

What is this?

When someone pays, Polar sends your app a webhook: a message signed with a secret. Your app must check the signature so nobody can fake a payment. Paste a webhook here to see the signature you should expect and whether it matches. It runs locally and nothing is uploaded. For safety, use a sandbox or test secret.
