Compare payment platform fees

What is this?

Who it's for: anyone about to sell a digital product, such as an app, template or course. When you'd use it: before choosing a payment platform or setting a price. Enter a price and expected sales to estimate what you'd receive after each platform's listed fees. That figure is your proceeds, not profit: it doesn't subtract hosting, tools, refunds, payout fees or taxes.
