What will you actually take home?

What is this?

When you sell through a merchant of record like Polar, they take a fee on every sale for handling payment and tax. Enter your price and expected sales to see what you keep on each platform.

PlatformFee / saleYou keep / saleYou keep / month

Polar uses its Starter plan: 5% + $0.50 per sale, plus 1.5% on non-US cards (polar.sh pricing, checked 2026-10-12). It excludes payout fees (about $2/month + 0.25% + $0.25 per payout) and the $15 dispute fee. Lemon Squeezy (5% + $0.50) and Gumroad (12.9% + $0.80) rates are as of Sep 2026.
